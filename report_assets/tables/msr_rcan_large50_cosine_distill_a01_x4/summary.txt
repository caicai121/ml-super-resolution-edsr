MSR-RCAN-large-50-cosine-distill-a0.1 Teacher Distillation Ablation
======================================================================

Teacher: RCAN-pretrained (Y+crop 32.52 dB)
Student: MSR-RCAN-large 8g8b (Y+crop 31.28 dB)
Alpha: 0.1
Loss: L_total = L1(SR_student, HR) + 0.1 * L1(SR_student, SR_teacher)

Training: 50 epochs, best at epoch 48
Best Val Y+crop PSNR: 30.75 dB

Test Results (70 images):
  RGB PSNR:  29.54 dB | RGB SSIM:  0.7858
  Y+crop PSNR: 31.16 dB | Y+crop SSIM: 0.8181

Comparison:
  MSR-RCAN-large-50-cosine (baseline):  31.28 dB
  Distill alpha=0.1:                     31.16 dB  (-0.12 dB)
  RCAN-pretrained (teacher):             32.52 dB

Conclusion: Teacher distillation INEFFECTIVE.
Direct L1 distillation on final SR output did not improve student performance.
Teacher (standard RCAN 10g20b) and student (MSRRCANV2 8g8b + MSRCAB + Deep Refine)
have structural differences leading to distribution mismatch.
Soft supervision conflicts with HR hard supervision.
Not pursuing alpha=0.2.
Best 50-epoch model remains MSR-RCAN-large-50-cosine at 31.28 dB.
